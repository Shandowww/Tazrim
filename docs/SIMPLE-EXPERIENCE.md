# Budgit: clear, light, everyday use

Tal's direction, 2026-10-20: fewer decisions, shorter explanations, gentle visual summaries, and useful next steps. Keep real amounts accessible, distinguish missing information from zero, and reserve questions for choices that change financial meaning. Keep Hebrew, RTL, the existing soft palette, accessible touch controls and explicit saving.

## Uploading reports

1. Open **מרכז הדוחות** and choose the files.
2. Check the recognized account/card. Budgit remembers confident matches; change them only when necessary.
3. Choose **קליטת הדוחות ושמירה**.

Categories are optional. Unknown card merchants are imported under **לסיווג**, while known categories remain intact. The optional category section can be opened during import. Later, use **אפשר גם אחר כך → בחירת קטגוריות** in Expenses. Choose a category next to a purchase, then **שמור שינויים**. Category changes preserve source amounts and do not create another expense.

Unreadable files, invalid bank rows and inconsistent card totals are rejected before uploading the source. They show **לא נקלט** with a brief next step; remove the file and download a fresh report. Other unresolved choices still stop the final batch save, so the app never announces a partially successful save. A source awaiting a required account or duplicate check is an unfinished upload, not a completed import.

There is no archive action in the normal interface. Removing a newly loaded, unimported source removes it from the inbox, without changing the budget or the file on the device. Successfully imported sources remain protected. Existing archived records are retained for compatibility and shown as **לא נקלט** in Settings; opening or uploading them again resumes the saved work without an archive-choice prompt. Existing backups remain intact.

## A lighter overview

Status shows a compact six-month chart titled **הוצאות לפי חודש** when at least two months contain expense details. Each month is a visible button with a border, an SVG forward arrow that is not mirrored by RTL text, and a selected state. Opening a month selects that exact Expenses month and shows **חזרה לסטטוס**. A browser-history entry enables native Back/Forward, including the iPhone edge-back gesture where supported. Back restores the Status chart position and prior month; unfinished monthly, detail, savings and privacy edits retain their existing navigation guards. The selected month’s full amount is shown once above the chart; individual bars do not squeeze currency labels into narrow columns. Missing months have disabled buttons. Status does not contain the Report Center tile, card-charge/pending-payment summaries, or the trends tile. When a card report is due, Status first shows a small **תזכורת להעלאת דוח** tile with the card, billing month/date, and **העלאת הדוח** button. It begins on the billing day, stays for the latest charged cycle until successful import (or confirmed no transactions), and requires no push permission. Ten-day advance push and its controls are retired. Manual totals and unfinished/failed uploads do not dismiss it. Below any reminder, Status starts with the individual account cards, followed by the spending chart and a separate **החיסכון שלי** tile with the reported savings balance, optional allocation details, and a link to Goals. The duplicate combined **מצב היתרות** section is removed. Expenses repeats the chart as a month selector instead of a dropdown: choosing a bar updates the category breakdown and card snapshots in place, without adding a navigation step. Swipeable six-month slides keep other months accessible through the same circular-arrow and position-dot design as the wallet cards, and missing-detail months show a gap and an empty breakdown rather than zero spending. The Expenses selector remains available with one or no months of details. Card charges stay in Expenses. Selecting the card surface opens **עסקאות בדוח** in a closable popup for the selected billing month: purchase date, original merchant and amount charged in shekels. It reads original report rows once, preserving refunds and installment notes; merchant aliases and category splits do not change this report view. Long lists offer **עוד עסקאות**. If only a total was saved, the popup explains that the report details have not been imported. The original data and monthly totals are not edited. Trends and deposit-planning controls are available under the manual-entry hub → חסכונות והשקעות → מגמות בולטות ותכנון הפקדות. Report Center remains in Settings and the menu. A short caption identifies imported details and incomplete data; missing months stay gaps. The savings tile includes compact goal rows: description, actual allocation progress, remaining deposit count at the configured monthly amount, optional deadline and a pace badge. Pace uses the configured amount without returns, not observed income or simulated edits; no date or rate remains explicitly unknown. Each row opens that exact goal. Missing-payer warnings include a direct **שיוך לחשבון** action that expands and focuses the exact card field in account settings; the calculator and card tile offer the same repair route. No-transactions confirmation reuses unambiguous saved source payers. Predicted savings and incomplete reports never trigger celebrations. Balance figures remain available in smaller type with their existing drill-down details.

Keep duplicate checks, unknown payers, protected full-month records and incomplete billing-cycle checks. These affect amounts and ownership; replacing them with guesses would make the interface appear easier while corrupting the result. Optional categorization is different and must not block import. No budgets are merged or selected silently.

## Personal colors

**הגדרות אפליקציה → מראה → צבע האפליקציה** offers five soft accents. Changes apply immediately and stay on this device, independently of System/Light/Dark and budget saving. **חשבונות הבנק → צבע החשבון** sets a color for the selected bank account; save it with the existing budget controls. Linked cards and account markers reuse it. Names remain visible so color is never the only identification cue. Automatic colors keep stable defaults; renaming an account does not change a chosen color.

## References and ongoing design rules

- [Copilot dashboard](https://help.copilot.money/en/articles/6045480-dashboard-tab-overview): a quick overview with detail available when requested.
- [Copilot cash flow](https://help.copilot.money/en/articles/9682232-cash-flow-tab-overview): changes over time and chart-based exploration.
- [Monarch manual import](https://help.monarchmoney.com/hc/en-us/articles/4409682789908-Import-Transaction-Data-Manually-from-Banks-or-Other-Finance-Apps): categories are optional during import.

Use these patterns, not copied branding. Prefer one primary action, recognized defaults, optional detail disclosures, friendly language and evidence-based suggestions. Do not conceal uncertainty, real deficits or failed saves. Technical backup and encryption controls belong in Settings rather than routine import decisions.

## Investment deposits

Status → **הפקדות להשקעות**, below savings, shows this month’s reported investment contributions and the total reported so far. Only actual past/current records count; confirmed account records can contribute when the combined month is incomplete. Combined totals and their account records are never added together. An unreported month says **טרם דווח**, while a confirmed zero stays zero. These are deposits, not portfolio value: returns and withdrawals are excluded. The bar chart covers 12 calendar months through the current Israel month, including the previous year when needed. Select a month to read its exact amount; unreported months stay gaps, confirmed zero remains zero, and striped bars identify partial account reporting. The selected month’s six-month slide stays visible. Both investment and expense charts use the wallet’s circular arrows and position dots, with native horizontal swipe, keyboard navigation and reduced-motion support. Browsing a slide does not change financial selection until a month is tapped; date ranges identify year boundaries. **עדכון הפקדות** opens the selected month’s deposit fields directly, preserving unfinished edits. Savings and goals remain separate; no balances or source records are changed.
